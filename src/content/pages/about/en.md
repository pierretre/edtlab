# About

## About the EDT Research Program

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program is a comprehensive initiative focused on advancing digital twin technology for engineering applications.

## Mission

Our mission is to develop innovative methodologies, foster collaboration between academia and industry, and advance the state of the art in engineering simulation and digital twin technology.

## Vision

To become a leading research program in digital twin technology, contributing to the advancement of engineering practices and industrial applications.

## About This Website

This website serves as the central hub for information about the EDT research program, providing access to research outputs, project information, and collaboration opportunities.
