## Project Overview

PC1 focuses on the hybridization and management of heterogeneous models within digital twins. The project addresses the challenge of combining physical models and data-based models through systematic approaches, interfaces, and operators.

Digital twins rely on diverse models that vary across multiple dimensions: inference methods (inductive vs. deductive), modeling approaches (empirical vs. mechanistic), concerns (behavioral, safety, structural), purpose (descriptive vs. prescriptive), fidelity levels, and temporal aspects. This heterogeneity presents both challenges and opportunities for model hybridization.

## Scientific Topics

1. **Interface for Hybridization(s)**: Standardizing model manipulation and characterization
2. **Hybridization Operators**: Formalizing structural and behavioral patterns for model combination
3. **Interface and Composition Analysis**: Analyzing models, interfaces, and hybridization operators

## Main Objectives

- Providing appropriate interfaces to manipulate any models and their usages
- Simplifying the hybridization of models through dedicated operators
- Ensuring the wise manipulation of models and hybridization with uncertainty management

## Methodology

The project is structured into three cohesive work packages addressing multi-fidelity and uncertainty across all activities:

### WP1: Interface for Hybridization(s)
- Creating structured metadata information for model management
- Providing functional and extra-functional interfaces for model hybridization
- Enabling model mutation and adaptation capabilities

### WP2: Hybridization Operators
- Identifying and formalizing hybridization patterns
- Developing reusable, parameterized operators for model combination
- Specifying simulation workflows and model hybridization processes

### WP3: Interface and Composition Analysis
- Analyzing models to enrich their interfaces
- Identifying synergies and hybridization potential between different interfaces
- Analyzing hybridization operators to enhance resulting interfaces

## Expected Outcomes

- Homogeneous model interfaces for heterogeneous model types
- Systematic hybridization operators replacing ad-hoc approaches
- Enhanced reliability and uncertainty management in model combinations
- Validated concepts through diverse use cases mixing data-based and mechanistic models
