# Focused Projects

EDT is structured around five targeted projects (PC1 to PC5), each dedicated to a key aspect of digital twin technology. Closely interconnected, these projects share common use cases and build on each other's progress to collectively form an integrated and comprehensive platform for digital-twin engineering.
