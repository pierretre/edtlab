## Project Overview

- **PC1**: Model Hybridization and Management
- **PC2**: Architecture, Composability and Interoperability  
- **PC3**: Methodology, Development Life Cycle, and Languages
- **PC4**: Digital Coupling and Gateway
- **PC5**: Human-Digital Twin Interactions

## Project Structure

Each project has dedicated pages with detailed information about objectives, methodology, and outcomes. The projects are tightly interconnected, sharing use cases and building upon each other's foundations to create a comprehensive digital twin engineering platform.
