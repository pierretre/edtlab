# PC2: Architecture, Composability and Interoperability

## Project Overview

PC2 addresses the challenge of building modular Digital Twins that can be composed and federated, moving away from monolithic, ad-hoc implementations toward systematic, scalable architectures that enable industrial scaling of digital twin technology.

## Scientific Topics

1. **Modular Architecture for DTs**: Enabling flexible composition and variability management
2. **Composition of DTs**: Orchestration at deployment level for dynamic federation
3. **Interoperability of DT Services**: Knowledge graphs and semantic web technologies
4. **Scale, Fidelity and Uncertainty Management**: Handling compounding uncertainties across DT boundaries

## Main Objectives

- Modularization of DTs to enable flexible composition addressing variability, trustworthiness, economics, and ethics
- Composition of DTs at deployment level to allow orchestration and federation
- Interoperability of several DTs using Knowledge Graph, Ontology and Semantic Web technologies
- Fidelity and uncertainty management across composed digital twin systems
- Composition of services for data acquisition, scalable machine learning, and dynamic adaptation

## Methodology

The project is organized into three complementary work packages:

### WP2.1: DT Reference Architecture
- Unified meta-data theory for variability, fidelity and uncertainty management
- Reference architecture patterns for modular digital twin systems
- Foundation for systematic composition and interoperability

### WP2.2: DT Modularisation and Composability
- Building DTs from pre-existing components and reverse engineering
- Design-time (software components) and deployment-time (Systems of Systems) composition
- Evolution management for neuromorphic and adaptive systems

### WP2.3: DT Semantic Interoperability
- Semantic web technologies for ontology alignment between independent DTs
- Knowledge Graph quality improvement and validation
- Semantic tracking of digital twin experiments and interactions

## Expected Outcomes

- Reference architecture for modular digital twin systems
- Composition frameworks for both design-time and runtime federation
- Semantic interoperability solutions using knowledge graphs and ontologies
- Uncertainty propagation methods across composed digital twin systems
- Validated approaches through use cases requiring interoperability and federation
