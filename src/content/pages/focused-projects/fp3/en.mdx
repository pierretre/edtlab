## Project Overview

PC3 develops specialized methodologies, development life cycles, and domain-specific languages for engineering digital twins. The project addresses the multidisciplinary nature of DT development, integrating AI, MBSE practices, and collaborative engineering approaches.

## Scientific Topics

1. **Methodology and Life Cycle**: Model, view & viewpoint, architecture framework, reverse engineering, and domain-specific languages
2. **Continuous Engineering, Co-evolution, and Quality**: Co-evolution, live-modeling, continuous integration, requirement engineering, VVUQ methods
3. **Collaborative Engineering and Augmented IDE**: Space-time collaboration, concurrent engineering, AI & GenAI integration, human-bot inclusion
4. **Deployment and Sustainability**: Continuous deployment, edge/cloud deployment, sustainability assessment, SustainableDevOps

## Main Objectives

- Develop a domain-specific modeling language for engineering digital twins throughout their lifecycle
- Define stakeholders, concerns, and viewpoints needed for comprehensive DT engineering coverage
- Specify and design a sustainable collaborative IDE integrating all viewpoints and enabling co-engineering
- Integrate analysis tools early in design phases with "analysis-in-the-loop" capabilities

## Methodology

The project is structured into four interconnected work packages:

### WP3.1: Methodology and Life Cycle
- Domain-specific language development (SysML V2 extension)
- Architecture framework definition following ISO42010
- Reverse engineering methodologies for existing systems

### WP3.2: Continuous Engineering, Co-evolution, and Quality
- Co-evolution management between physical and digital twins
- Live-modeling and programming approaches
- Continuous integration and VVUQ (Verification, Validation, Uncertainty Quantification) methods
- Conformity assessment automation using AI and MBSE

### WP3.3: Collaborative Engineering and Augmented IDE
- Collaborative development environments for multidisciplinary teams
- AI-augmented IDE with human-bot inclusion capabilities
- Concurrent and agile engineering methodologies

### WP3.4: Deployment and Sustainability
- Continuous deployment strategies for edge and cloud environments
- Environmental impact assessment frameworks
- Sustainable development operations (SustainableDevOps)
- Analysis-in-the-loop integration for real-time decision support

## Expected Outcomes

- Domain-specific modeling language for digital twin engineering (Artemis-DSL)
- Comprehensive architecture framework (Artemis-AF) following ISO42010
- Collaborative augmented IDE (Artemis-IDE) with AI integration
- Sustainability assessment framework for digital twin lifecycle
- Validated methodologies through industrial use cases
