# PhD Students Club membership

To join the club, fill in the form below. Your application will be forwarded to the programme team for review. Registration requires a short statement of motivation, confirmation of the PhD supervisor's approval and EDT programme validation.

Before registering, please [read the club charter](/media/phds-club/membership_charter_phd_students_club.pdf).
