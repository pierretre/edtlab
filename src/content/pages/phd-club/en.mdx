# PhD Students Club Membership application

The EDT PhD students Club brings together the PhD candidates of the Engineering Digital Twins program, as well as any PhD student working in France on digital twins that registers and respects the EDT PhD students charter.

Its mission is to foster a vibrant scientific community, encourage peer exchanges, and support young researchers throughout their doctoral journey, despite the geographic dispersion of host teams. The club organizes monthly seminars, an annual day, and runs a space for sharing and mutual support open to all.
