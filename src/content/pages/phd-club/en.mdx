# PhD Students Club

The EDT PhD Students Club brings together the PhD candidates of the Engineering Digital Twins programme, as well as any PhD student working in France on digital twins who registers and respects the club charter.

Its mission is to foster a vibrant scientific community, encourage peer exchanges, and support young researchers throughout their doctoral journey, despite the geographic dispersion of host teams. The club organises monthly seminars, an annual day, and runs a space for sharing and mutual support open to all.

👉 [Join the club](#membership)

## What the club offers

- **Club seminars.** Regular short seminars where members present their work in a low-stakes setting and receive feedback from peers, alongside shared readings and informal exchanges.
- **EDT scientific events.** Access to the EDT programme's summer schools, scientific seminars and workshops, and the annual PhD Students Club Day — as participants and, when relevant, as speakers.
- **Mailing list and communication channels.** A dedicated mailing list for sharing references, calls for help, opportunities and announcements; complemented by a chat platform and a shared file space.
- **A network.** Connections with PhD students working on engineering digital twins across French institutions, with EDT supervisors, and with the programme's industrial and academic partners.
- **Alumni continuity.** At the end of the PhD, an invitation to join the club's alumni mailing list.

## Five principles

The club recognises itself in five principles that its members undertake to embody in their interactions: **listening**, **benevolence**, **sincerity**, **reciprocity** and **curiosity**.

## Who can join

Membership is open to any PhD student enrolled in a French graduate school whose work bears on engineering digital twins, broadly understood. Being funded by the EDT programme is not a requirement. Membership lasts for the duration of the PhD.
