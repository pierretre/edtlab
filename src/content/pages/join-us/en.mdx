# Join Us

Explore career opportunities and open positions within the EDT research program. We are always looking for talented researchers, engineers, and students to join our interdisciplinary team working on cutting-edge digital twin technologies.

<JobOfferList lang="en" />

## Position Types

We offer various types of positions across our research projects:

- **Postdoctoral Researchers**: Advanced research positions for recent PhD graduates
- **PhD Students**: Doctoral research opportunities in collaboration with partner universities
- **Research Engineers**: Technical positions for software development and system integration
- **Internships**: Short-term positions for students and early-career professionals

## Application Process

To apply for any position:

1. **Review the position details** by clicking on the job offer cards above
2. **Prepare your application** including CV, cover letter, and relevant documents
3. **Submit your application** following the instructions in each job posting
4. **Follow up** if you don't hear back within the specified timeframe
