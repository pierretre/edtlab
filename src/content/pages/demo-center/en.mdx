# Demo Center

## Overview

The EDT Demo Center showcases practical applications and demonstrators developed through our research program. With over 40 use cases spanning natural systems, human-made systems, and artificial systems, we demonstrate the real-world impact of digital twin technology across diverse engineering domains.

## Use Case Categories

### Natural Systems
Digital twins of environmental and ecological systems including:
- Climate and weather modeling systems
- Ecosystem monitoring and management
- Natural resource management
- Environmental impact assessment

### Anthropized Systems
Human-modified systems and urban environments:
- Smart city infrastructure
- Transportation networks
- Urban planning and development
- Public service optimization

### Engineered Systems

#### Cyber-Physical Systems
- Industrial automation and control systems
- Autonomous vehicle systems
- Robotics and manufacturing systems
- IoT-enabled smart devices

#### Infrastructure Systems
- Energy distribution networks
- Telecommunications infrastructure
- Water and waste management systems
- Building and facility management

#### Process Systems
- Manufacturing and production processes
- Supply chain and logistics
- Quality control and optimization
- Maintenance and lifecycle management

## End-to-End Demonstrators

Five representative end-to-end demonstrators validate the integrated EDT platform:

### Territory Scale Demonstrator
- **Scope**: City/area digital twin reconstruction
- **Features**: Virtual human population modeling, urban planning simulation
- **Applications**: Smart city development, traffic optimization, emergency response planning

### Building/Factory Scale Demonstrator
- **Scope**: Reconfigurable assembly line digital twin
- **Features**: AR worker augmentation, VR expert monitoring, remote assistance
- **Applications**: Industry 4.0, worker training, production optimization

### Technical Object Scale Demonstrator
- **Scope**: Complex system digital twins (vehicles, satellites, machinery)
- **Features**: VR maintenance training, AR-assisted physical operations
- **Applications**: Maintenance planning, operator training, predictive maintenance

### Network Infrastructure Demonstrator
- **Scope**: Digital twin of IT and communication networks
- **Features**: Real-time monitoring, performance optimization, security management
- **Applications**: Network management, capacity planning, fault detection

### Environmental Monitoring Demonstrator
- **Scope**: Earth observation and environmental systems
- **Features**: Multi-scale modeling, data assimilation, prediction systems
- **Applications**: Climate monitoring, disaster management, resource planning
