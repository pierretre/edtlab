# EDT Research Program

## Program Overview

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program aims to advance the field of digital twin technology for engineering applications.

## Goals and Objectives

- Develop innovative digital twin methodologies
- Foster collaboration between academia and industry
- Advance the state of the art in engineering simulation

## Program Structure

The program is organized into focused projects (PC1-PC5) that address specific aspects of digital twin technology.
