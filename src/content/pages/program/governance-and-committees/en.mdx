# Governance and Committees

## Governance

The governance of the EDT program defines strategy, coordinates focused projects and cross-cutting actions, and liaises with funders and partners.

## Scientific Committee

The Scientific Committee advises on scientific directions, evaluates outputs and ensures research quality.

## Usage Committee

The Usage Committee gathers end-users and stakeholders to guide use-case priorities and demonstrators.

## Technical Committee

The Technical Committee handles integration, architecture choices (Artemis) and implementation best practices.
