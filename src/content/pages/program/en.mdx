# EDT Research Program

## Program Overview

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program aims to advance the field of digital twin technology for engineering applications, creating a multidisciplinary community with international leadership in digital twin engineering.

## Vision

The engineering of digital twins requires a multi-disciplinary effort at the crossroads of software engineering, modeling and simulation, numerical analysis, artificial intelligence, computing systems (HPC, cloud/edge), distributed and embedded systems, HCI, and data management. EDT provides a unique opportunity to foster collaborations between these different scientific communities, creating a multidisciplinary community with international leadership.

## Goals and Objectives

EDT aims to achieve four main goals:

1. **Goal 1**: Create a national scientific, technological and user community with international leadership on Engineering Digital Twins
2. **Goal 2**: Develop the required foundations of the science and engineering of digital twins
3. **Goal 3**: Deliver Artemis, a collaborative and web-based software platform to engineer digital twins
4. **Goal 4**: Develop end-to-end EDT Demonstrators to experiment and validate the Artemis platform

## Program Structure

The program is organized into five tightly connected focused projects (PC1-PC5) based on a collection of more than 40 shared use cases, and 7 cross-cutting actions:

### Focused Projects
- **PC1**: Model Hybridization and Management
- **PC2**: Architecture, Composability and Interoperability
- **PC3**: Methodology, Development Life Cycle, and Languages
- **PC4**: Digital Coupling and Gateway
- **PC5**: Human-Digital Twin Interactions

### Cross-Cutting Actions
- **AT1**: Animation and Community Building
- **AT2**: Use Cases and End-to-end Demonstrators
- **AT3**: Platform Integration
- **AT6**: Ethics and Sustainability
- **AT7**: Dissemination and Exploitation

## Scientific Barriers

EDT addresses six major scientific challenges:

- **C1**: Methods and abstractions for combining physical and data-based models
- **C2**: Composing different DTs to reflect Systems of Systems (SoS)
- **C3**: Digital twin software foundations and architecture
- **C4**: Bidirectional flows between physical and digital twins
- **C5**: Human-computer interaction and multimodal data fusion
- **C6**: Ethical and environmental responsibility considerations

## Committee and Governance

The program is governed by an Operating Committee and guided by Usage and Scientific Committees, ensuring close partnership with related French PEPR programs and international collaborations.

## Partners and Collaborators

EDT brings together leading French research institutions including Inria, CNRS, CEA, universities, and industrial partners, with connections to international research communities and standardization bodies.

## Expected Outcomes

- A methodological corpus for the Science of Engineering Digital Twins
- An open-source platform (Artemis) for Engineering Digital Twins
- Generic open-source tools and libraries
- Representative end-to-end demonstrators of selected use cases
