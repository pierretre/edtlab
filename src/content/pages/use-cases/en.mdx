# Use Cases

The EDT Research Program will publicly provide and maintain a **comprehensive (and living) catalog of use cases focused on the engineering of digital twins**. This catalog will showcase real-world applications, methodologies, and best practices developed across research and industry collaborations. By sharing these examples, the program aims to support innovation, guide practitioners in designing and deploying digital twins, and foster a broader community of knowledge around advanced digital twin engineering.
