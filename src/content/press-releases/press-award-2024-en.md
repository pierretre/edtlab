## Press Release - September 20, 2024

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program has been awarded the prestigious European Innovation Award for Excellence in Digital Technologies at the annual European Research Excellence Awards ceremony held in Brussels.

## Award Recognition

The award recognizes the EDT team's groundbreaking work in developing real-time synchronization algorithms for industrial digital twins, which has the potential to transform manufacturing processes across Europe.

## Breakthrough Research

The winning research, led by Dr. Jean-Pierre Moreau and his team at the EDT program, focuses on:

### Real-Time Synchronization
Novel algorithms that enable digital twins to synchronize with physical systems in real-time, reducing latency to under 10 milliseconds.

### Scalable Architecture
A distributed computing framework that can handle thousands of concurrent digital twin instances across multiple industrial sites.

### Predictive Maintenance
Advanced machine learning models that predict equipment failures up to 30 days in advance with 95% accuracy.

## Industry Impact

The research has already been implemented in pilot projects with several major manufacturers, resulting in:

- 25% reduction in unplanned downtime
- 15% improvement in energy efficiency
- 30% faster product development cycles
- €2.3 million in cost savings across pilot sites

## Award Committee Statement

"The EDT program's research represents a significant leap forward in digital twin technology," said Prof. Elena Rodriguez, Chair of the Award Committee. "Their work demonstrates exceptional innovation and practical impact that will benefit industries across Europe."

## Research Team

The award-winning team includes:

- **Dr. Jean-Pierre Moreau** - Principal Investigator
- **Dr. Sarah Chen** - Lead Algorithm Developer
- **Dr. Marco Rossi** - Industrial Applications Specialist
- **Dr. Anna Kowalski** - Machine Learning Expert

## Future Developments

Building on this success, the EDT program plans to:

- Expand research into quantum-enhanced digital twins
- Develop industry-specific digital twin standards
- Launch a European digital twin certification program
- Establish new research partnerships across the EU

## Recognition Ceremony

The award was presented by European Commissioner for Innovation, Research, Culture, Education and Youth, Mariya Gabriel, at a ceremony attended by over 500 researchers, industry leaders, and policymakers.

## About the Award

The European Innovation Award for Excellence in Digital Technologies is presented annually to recognize outstanding research contributions that advance digital transformation across European industries. This year's competition included over 200 submissions from 28 countries.
