## Press Release - February 10, 2024

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program has been awarded significant additional funding to support the expansion of its research activities into Phase 2. This funding will enable the program to accelerate its research efforts and broaden its impact across multiple industrial sectors.

## Funding Details

The new funding package, totaling €15 million over three years, will support:

- Expansion of the five focused projects (PC1-PC5)
- Development of new industrial partnerships
- Enhancement of the demonstration platform
- International collaboration initiatives

## Program Director Statement

"This additional funding represents a strong vote of confidence in our research approach and the potential impact of digital twin technology," said Dr. Catherine Martin, EDT Program Director. "It will allow us to accelerate our timeline and expand our reach to new industrial applications."

## Research Impact

The EDT program has already demonstrated significant progress in its first phase, with:

- 25+ peer-reviewed publications
- 8 industrial partnerships established
- 3 patent applications filed
- 12 PhD students and postdocs trained

## Next Steps

Phase 2 activities will begin in April 2024, with new recruitment opportunities and expanded research facilities. The program continues to welcome collaboration proposals from academic and industrial partners.
