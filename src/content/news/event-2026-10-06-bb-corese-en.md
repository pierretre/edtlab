The **EDT** seminar will take place on **October 6, 2026, from 5:30 to 6:30 PM (CEST)**, by videoconference.

**Fabien Gandon** (Inria) will present the Building Block [**Corese**](https://project.inria.fr/corese/), a software platform for the Semantic Web that implements the W3C standards (RDF, RDFS, SPARQL, OWL, SHACL) to represent, query and reason over knowledge graphs.
