*Following its kick-off in October 2026 in Nancy, the targeted project **Synchronic** (PC4 of the EDT programme) invites its community to its next scientific day on Thursday 1 April 2027.*

This day will be an opportunity to take stock of progress on the synchronisation and coupling of physical systems with their digital twins: first results from the work packages, presentations by PhD students and postdoctoral researchers, and discussions with the use case leaders.

It is aimed at members of the Synchronic consortium and, more broadly, at the EDT programme community.

- **Date:** Thursday 1 April 2027
