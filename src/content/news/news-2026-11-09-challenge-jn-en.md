*The EDT program is a partner of the first edition of the Digital Twin Challenge, launched by the French national academic Industrial Engineering community. Registration is open.*

The challenge invites academic teams, or mixed teams with industrial partners, to demonstrate on a real use case the implementation of a digital twin applied to a system producing goods or services. To take part, teams must go from concept to implementation and have a connected asset and digital twin.

Beyond bringing the community together, the initiative aims to document the state of national practices in digital twin engineering and to contribute to a shared reference framework. Results will be presented at the SAGIP congress in May 2027 in Troyes.

The challenge is organised with the CT JNUM-SAGIP, the GIS S.mart, AFIS, the EDT program and the Digital Twins for Industrial Systems sponsorship Chair (IMT).
