## Seminar Overview

This seminar explores the cutting-edge developments and future directions in digital twin technology, with a focus on engineering applications. Leading researchers and industry experts will share their insights on emerging trends and breakthrough innovations.

## Featured Speakers

- **Dr. Marie Dubois** - CNRS Research Director, Digital Systems
- **Prof. James Anderson** - MIT, Computational Engineering
- **Dr. Elena Rodriguez** - Siemens, Industrial IoT

## Topics Covered

- Next-generation digital twin architectures
- AI and machine learning integration
- Real-time simulation and optimization
- Sustainability and environmental impact
- Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing

## Format

This is a virtual seminar with live Q&A sessions. Participants will receive access links and presentation materials prior to the event.

## Registration

Free registration required. Continuing education credits available for professional engineers.

## Duration

2 hours including presentations and interactive discussion sessions.
