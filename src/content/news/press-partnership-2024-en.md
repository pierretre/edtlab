## Press Release - May 15, 2024

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) research program today announced strategic partnerships with three leading industrial companies to accelerate the development and deployment of digital twin technologies across manufacturing sectors.

## Partnership Overview

The new partnerships include:

- **Airbus**: Collaboration on aerospace digital twin applications
- **Schneider Electric**: Focus on industrial automation and energy management
- **Dassault Systèmes**: Advanced simulation and modeling platforms

## Collaborative Research Areas

The partnerships will focus on several key research areas:

### Aerospace Applications

Working with Airbus to develop next-generation digital twins for aircraft design, manufacturing, and maintenance processes.

### Industrial Automation

Partnering with Schneider Electric to create intelligent digital twins for smart manufacturing and energy optimization.

### Simulation Platforms

Collaborating with Dassault Systèmes to enhance 3D modeling and simulation capabilities for complex industrial systems.

## Expected Impact

These partnerships are expected to:

- Accelerate technology transfer from research to industry
- Create new job opportunities for researchers and engineers
- Establish France as a leader in digital twin technology
- Generate significant economic impact across multiple sectors

## Industry Statements

"This collaboration with the EDT program represents a significant step forward in our digital transformation journey," said Marie Dubois, Chief Technology Officer at Airbus. "Digital twin technology will revolutionize how we design and manufacture aircraft."

## Program Benefits

The partnerships will provide:

- Access to real-world industrial data and use cases
- Co-funding for research projects totaling €8 million
- Internship and job opportunities for students and researchers
- Joint publication and patent opportunities

## Next Steps

The first joint research projects will begin in September 2024, with initial results expected by the end of the year. Regular progress reviews will ensure alignment with both academic and industrial objectives.
