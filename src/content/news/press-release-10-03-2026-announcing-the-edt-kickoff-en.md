We are pleased to announce that the official **kick-off** of the **EDT** (Engineering Digital Twins) program will take place on **June 24, 2026** at INRIA's premises in Paris.

This inaugural event will convene all program partners and collaborators, alongside funding bodies ANR and the "Inria Agence Programme Numérique", to present a comprehensive overview of EDT's scientific objectives, governance structure, and research roadmap.

Launched on January 1, 2026 for a duration of six years with a total budget of €17 million, the EDT program is a **national research initiative** aimed at establishing **the scientific and technological foundations** of Engineering Digital Twins — spanning model hybridization, software architecture, digital coupling, sustainable development lifecycles, and human-DT interaction.
