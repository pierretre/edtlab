We are pleased to announce that the official **kick-off ** of the **EDT** (Engineering Digital Twins) program will take place on June 24, 2026 at INRIA, Paris.

This inaugural event will convene **all program partners and collaborators**, alongside funding bodies **ANR** and the **Inria Numérique program agency**, to present a comprehensive overview of **EDT's scientific objectives**, governance structure, and research roadmap.

The meeting will mark the formal launch of a national research initiative aimed at establishing **the scientific and technological foundations of Engineering Digital Twins** — spanning model hybridization, software architecture, digital coupling, sustainable development lifecycles, and human-DT interaction.
