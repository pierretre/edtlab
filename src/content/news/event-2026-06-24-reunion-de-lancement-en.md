As part of its mission as a Programs Agency for Digital Technology, Algorithms, Software and Uses, Inria is pleased to launch the EDT research program - Engineering Digital Twins.

The program aims to bring together scientific, institutional and industrial partners around emblematic use cases, in order to ground scientific work in real-world conditions and to foster the adoption of the software tools proposed by researchers.

The Engineering Digital Twins (EDT) program, funded by the French National Research Agency under France 2030 (ANR-25-APJN-0001) and operated by the Inria Digital Agency, is holding its official kick-off meeting.

This day will bring together all the institutional, scientific and industrial partners of the program around presentations of its objectives and research areas, and will be a first key opportunity for exchanges between the teams involved in this national initiative.
