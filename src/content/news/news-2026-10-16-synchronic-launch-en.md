*On 15 and 16 October 2026, in Nancy, the targeted project **Synchronic** (PC4 of the EDT programme) officially kicked off. Its challenge: keeping a physical system and its digital twin synchronised in a verifiable, frugal and safe way.*

**Thursday afternoon: context, use cases and scientific challenges**

Benoit Combemale opened the two days with a presentation of the EDT programme and its ecosystem. The targeted use case leaders then presented the real-world systems on which the project's work will be anchored:

- UC05 "Fluidic Loop" – CETIM (Hubert Lejeune);
- UC25 "CO₂ Storage" – IFPEN (Véronique Gervais, Ainhoa Lincot and Abir El-Feki);
- UC Drone – Inria and LORIA (Laurent Ciarletta and Liliana Cucu-Grosjean);
- UC Ephemeral Factory Lab – CRAN (Hind Bril El Haouzi).

The work package leaders presented the project's scientific challenges, along with the associated PhD theses and postdoctoral positions.

**Friday morning: "interactions and pooling" workshop**

This workshop made it possible to link the work packages to the use cases and to bring out the first collaborations.

**What's happened since?**

- Recruitment is progressing: a new PhD thesis on the monitoring and formal verification of digital twins has just been approved, co-supervised between UGA (LIG) and CRAN.
- Targeted meetings with the use case leaders are being scheduled.
- The project's next scientific day will take place on **1 April 2027**.
