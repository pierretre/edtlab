The first EDT seminar took place on **April 7, 2026**.

**Benoit CAILLAUD** (Inria/Hycomes) presented the Building Block [**Isamdae**](https://team.inria.fr/hycomes/software/isamdae/), a tool specialized in the structural analysis of systems of differential-algebraic equations (DAE). This block is essential to ensure the robustness of the simulation of complex physical models.
