The **EDT** seminar took place on **June 2, 2026, from 5:30 to 6:30 PM (CEST)**, by videoconference.

**Pierre Alliez** (Inria) presented the Building Block [**CGAL**](https://www.cgal.org/) (Computational Geometry Algorithms Library), which provides validated implementations for processing complex spatial data: meshing (2D/3D), surface reconstruction, convex hulls, Voronoi diagrams and spatial search structures.
