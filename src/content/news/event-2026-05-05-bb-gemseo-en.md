The **EDT** seminar will take place on **May 5, 2026, from 3:30 to 4:30 PM (UTC)**, by videoconference.

**Jean-Christophe Giret** (IRT Saint-Exupéry) will present the Building Block [**Gemseo**](https://gemseo.readthedocs.io/en/stable/), an open-source Python library dedicated to automating multidisciplinary processes, with a particular focus on the optimization of complex systems coupling several physical domains.
