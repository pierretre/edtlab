The **EDT** seminar took place on **May 5.

**Jean-Christophe Giret** (IRT Saint-Exupéry) presented the Building Block [**Gemseo**](https://gemseo.readthedocs.io/en/stable/), an open-source Python library dedicated to automating multidisciplinary processes, with a particular focus on the optimization of complex systems coupling several physical domains.
