The **EDT PhD Students Club** will hold its day on **December 8, 2026**.

This day will bring together the PhD candidates of the Engineering Digital Twins program, as well as PhD students working in France on digital twins who are members of the club. It will be an opportunity to:

- present and discuss ongoing doctoral work;
- meet the other PhD students of the program despite the geographic dispersion of host teams;
- exchange with the researchers and partners of the EDT program.

## Program

- 09:00-09:30: Welcome coffee
- 09:30-10:00: Introduction (EDT scientific directors)
- 10:00-11:00: My PhD in 1 slide (PhD students)
- 11:00-12:00: Research Speed Dating - Find Your Research Twin! (PhD students)
- 12:00-13:30: Lunch
- 13:30-15:00: Let’s Build Our PhD Club! (PhD students)
- 15:00-15:30: Debrief (PhD students) & Conclusion (EDT scientific directors)
- 15:30-16:00: Break
- 16:00-18:00: Social Event
