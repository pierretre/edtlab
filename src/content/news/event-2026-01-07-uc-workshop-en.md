The first sharing seminar between Use Case (UC) providers and scientific partners of the EDT programme was held on Tuesday 6 and Wednesday 7 January 2026 at ENS de Lyon. Around thirty Use Cases were presented and discussed over the two days, in front of nearly one hundred participants.
