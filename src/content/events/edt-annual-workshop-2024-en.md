## Event Overview

Join us for the inaugural EDT Annual Workshop, a premier gathering of researchers, industry partners, and stakeholders in the field of engineering digital twins.

## Program Highlights

### Day 1: Research Presentations
- **Keynote**: "The Future of Digital Twin Technology" by Prof. Jean-Marc Jézéquel
- **PC1 Session**: Model Hybridization and Management advances
- **PC2 Session**: Architecture and Interoperability developments
- **Poster Session**: Latest research findings from PhD students

### Day 2: Industry Applications
- **Industry Panel**: Digital Twin Applications in Manufacturing
- **Use Case Presentations**: Real-world implementations and lessons learned
- **PC3 Session**: Development methodologies and tools
- **Networking Reception**: Collaboration opportunities

### Day 3: Demonstrators and Future Directions
- **Live Demonstrations**: Artemis platform and end-to-end demonstrators
- **PC4 & PC5 Sessions**: Digital coupling and human interaction advances
- **Roadmap Discussion**: Future research directions and priorities
