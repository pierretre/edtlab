## Workshop Overview

Join us for an intensive hands-on workshop focused on the practical implementation of digital twin technology in industrial environments. This workshop brings together researchers, engineers, and industry professionals to explore real-world applications and share best practices.

## Program

### Morning Session (9:00 - 12:00)
- Introduction to Digital Twin Fundamentals
- Case Studies from Manufacturing Industries
- Interactive Demonstrations

### Afternoon Session (13:30 - 17:00)
- Hands-on Implementation Lab
- Group Projects and Collaboration
- Results Presentation and Discussion

## Target Audience

This workshop is designed for:
- Research engineers and scientists
- Industrial automation professionals
- Graduate students in engineering
- Technology managers and decision makers

## Registration

Limited to 30 participants. Registration includes workshop materials, lunch, and networking reception.

## Prerequisites

Basic knowledge of industrial systems and programming concepts recommended but not required.
