## Context

Digital twins are virtual representations of real-world products, systems, or processes, enabling simulation, integration, testing, monitoring, and maintenance. They play a pivotal role in optimizing performance, reliability, and adaptability across industries.
The Engineering Digital Twin [EDT program](https://edtlab.fr/en/), funded by the France 2030 investment plan, is a national initiative aimed at advancing the scientific and technical foundations of digital twins. It brings together leading academic (Inria, CEA, CNRS, etc...) and industrial partners to develop open-source tools, methodologies, and the Artemis collaborative platform for digital twin engineering.
