## Position Overview

We are seeking an exceptional Postdoctoral Researcher to join our PC4 (Digital Coupling and Gateway) team. This position focuses on advancing the state-of-the-art in bidirectional data flows between physical systems and their digital twins, with emphasis on real-time synchronization, data assimilation, and network optimization.

## Research Focus

The successful candidate will work on cutting-edge research addressing:

- **Smart Data Collection**: Developing strategies for efficient data collection, optimal sensor placement, and sustainable data processing
- **Real-time Synchronization**: Ensuring accurate synchronization between digital twins and physical systems under various network conditions
- **Data Assimilation**: Implementing advanced techniques for integrating real-world data into digital models
- **Network Optimization**: Co-optimizing digital twins and network systems for improved performance

## Key Responsibilities

- Conduct innovative research on digital coupling mechanisms
- Develop and validate novel algorithms for real-time data synchronization
- Implement prototypes and proof-of-concept systems
- Collaborate with industrial partners on use case validation
- Publish research findings in top-tier conferences and journals
- Mentor PhD students and contribute to project deliverables
- Participate in international collaborations and workshops

## Required Qualifications

- PhD in Computer Science, Control Systems, Electrical Engineering, or closely related field
- Strong theoretical and practical background in real-time systems
- Experience with IoT protocols and edge computing architectures
- Demonstrated research excellence through publications
- Proficiency in programming (Python, C++, or similar)
- Excellent written and oral communication skills in English

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with digital twin technologies
- Knowledge of 5G/6G networks and their applications
- Background in complex event processing or data assimilation
- Familiarity with cloud-edge continuum architectures
- Experience with formal verification methods
- Understanding of cyber-physical systems
- French language skills (beneficial but not required)

## Research Environment

You will join the EDT (Engineering Digital Twins) program, a major French research initiative bringing together leading institutions including INRIA, CNRS, CEA, and top universities. The position offers:

- Access to state-of-the-art research infrastructure
- Collaboration with world-class researchers
- Engagement with industrial partners on real-world applications
- Opportunities for international mobility and networking
- Competitive salary according to French postdoc standards
- 2-year contract with possibility of extension

## About PC4

PC4 (Digital Coupling and Gateway) addresses the critical challenge of establishing reliable, efficient, and secure bidirectional communication between physical systems and their digital representations. Our research spans from theoretical foundations to practical implementations, with applications in smart cities, industrial systems, and critical infrastructure.
