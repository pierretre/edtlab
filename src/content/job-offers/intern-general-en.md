## Position Overview

Join our research team for a 6-month internship focused on digital twin technologies. This is an excellent opportunity for students to gain hands-on experience in cutting-edge research while contributing to real-world projects.

## Internship Details

- **Duration**: 6 months (flexible start date)
- **Type**: Full-time internship
- **Location**: Paris research facility
- **Supervision**: Direct mentorship from senior researchers

## Possible Research Areas

- Digital twin modeling and simulation
- Data integration and processing
- User interface development
- Performance optimization
- Literature review and state-of-the-art analysis

## What You'll Gain

- Hands-on experience with digital twin technologies
- Mentorship from experienced researchers
- Exposure to industrial partnerships
- Potential for publication opportunities
- Networking within the research community
- Internship stipend and benefits

## Application Process

Submit your application with:
- Current CV and academic transcript
- Brief cover letter (1 page max)
- One academic or professional reference
- Portfolio of projects (optional but recommended)

## Timeline

- **Application deadline**: November 30, 2024
- **Interview period**: December 2024
- **Start dates**: Flexible between January-June 2025
